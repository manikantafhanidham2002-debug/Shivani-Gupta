SECTION 09 · CRITICAL COMPLIANCE & CHECKLIST

Critical Warnings & Beginner Checklist

Avoid catastrophic compliance penalties and follow the golden 6-step roadmap for hassle-free shipments.

⚠️

Export Obligation Shortfall

Missing obligations under Advance Authorisation or EPCG means repaying all saved customs duties, with penal interest of up to 18% p.a., plus statutory compounding penalties.

🚨

FEMA Non-Realisation

Not receiving foreign currency payment within 9 months triggers automatic recovery and cancellation of any RoDTEP, RoSCTL or Duty Drawback benefits already claimed, plus RBI EDPMS flagging.

Step-by-Step Checklist for Beginners

6 Golden Steps
1
Foundational Registrations Secure your IEC, register your AD Code with customs at all shipping ports, and obtain your RCMC from your sector council.
2
Verify HS Code & Tariffs Verify your product's 8-digit ITC-HS Code on ICEGATE to confirm import/export tariffs, FTAs, and RoDTEP incentive rates.
3
Choose the Right Incoterm FOB or CIF are strongly recommended for beginners to balance costs, freight control, and clear risk handover points.
4
Secure Payment Guarantee Ensure overseas payment is backed by an Irrevocable Confirmed Letter of Credit (LC) or a 100% advance wire deposit (T/T).
5
Flawless Customs Filing Ensure your CHA files the Shipping Bill (export) or Bill of Entry (import) with 100% accurate invoice and packing list data.
6
Realise e-BRC Promptly Ensure your bank generates an electronic e-BRC immediately on receiving remittance to unlock tax refunds and close FEMA EDPMS.
